# Sound credits

Short UI sound effects used in ReadRight. Files here are served as-is at `/sounds/<file>`.

Guidelines:
- `.mp3`, short and small — button sounds under ~1 second / 30 KB; a one-off cheer (results) can be a few seconds / ~100 KB.
- Name files by what they're for, in kebab-case (e.g. `quiz-correct.mp3`, `results-reveal.mp3`).
- Never play during recording or the `/quality-check` noise check — the mic will pick it up.
